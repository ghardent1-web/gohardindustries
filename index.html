About Landrick Pond

Experience across music, film, technology and business.

Landrick Pond is an experienced film producer, director and music engineer with more than 25 years of work alongside actors, musicians and production professionals. His background includes feature films, short films, music videos, corporate events, commercials, recording sessions and studio productions.

He has served as a Film Producer/Director with P&W Film Productions since 2007 and as a Music Engineer with GO HARD INDUSTRIES Studios in Anderson, South Carolina. He also studied business at Denmark Technical College.
